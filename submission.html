Name and description

FORMA — a warm, editorial theme for furniture, homeware and made-to-order studios. Cream and ink palette, Manrope typography, oversized display headings and generous whitespace put the product in focus.

Short listing line, if the form asks for one:

A calm editorial storefront for furniture and board makers.

Feature list

  • Homepage hero as a single image or a slideshow of up to six slides, with per-slide copy, adjustable overlay and on-demand slide loading
  • Product row switchable between grid and drag-scrollable slider, with arrows and optional autoplay that pauses on hover, focus and tab blur
  • Collection pages with filtering, sorting and Dawn quick-add
  • Product page with variant picker, quantity rules, sticky add-to-cart and payment badges
  • Editorial blog index with two-column cards, fixed image height and excerpt
  • Ten custom homepage sections: hero, marquee, product row, categories, story, materials, gallery, testimonials, newsletter, journal
  • Five cream colour schemes, multi-column footer, collapsible mobile nav
  • Responsive from 320px up; WCAG AA contrast; prefers-reduced-motion respected
  • Self-hosted Manrope (SIL OFL), no third-party scripts
